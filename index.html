Ouro

Run AI coding agents on your own computers and talk to them from your phone.

Ouro keeps a team of long-running AI agents (Claude Code or Codex) on your own Macs and Linux machines. You talk to them from an iPhone app or the browser: give them tasks, answer their questions, and get their results. Agents work in their own git worktrees, message each other, and can even change Ouro itself. Ask for a feature from your phone, and an agent builds it and ships it to your server and your app.

Agents can also use tools on the computer they run on, such as a Gmail connection so they can search and read your mail and draft replies. They only send email after you approve it.
